Help & Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers for participants and event organizers

How do I register for an event?

Open any event category or click View All, then press Register on an open event and enter your User UUID.

Why does it say an event is at capacity?

Each event has a maximum seat limit enforced by the API. Once full, new registrations return a capacity notice.

How are leaderboard points calculated?

Students earn points for attending workshops, completing hackathons, and placing in top 3 competition ranks.

How can organizers view attendees?

Organizers can query /api/events/<id>/registrations?organizer_id=<uuid> or switch to Organizer view from the top-right menu.
